# Incorporated Analyzer Source Notices

This directory contains the third-party source material incorporated into Vibe Check's
Function Metrics analyzer. This notice describes only those translated sources.

## Lizard 1.24.0

Vibe Check contains modified TypeScript translations of source ranges from
[terryyin/lizard](https://github.com/terryyin/lizard), fixed at revision
`308b1c3efd8c1c69bcc3eb82deeaec64fd3662ec`. The exact source ranges, target paths,
and SPDX identities are recorded in [lizard-1.24.0-provenance.json](lizard-1.24.0-provenance.json).
The translated source headers identify their originating source and modification status.

- The translated Lizard source is covered by the complete
  [Lizard MIT text](Lizard-1.24.0-MIT.txt).
- Translated ranges from `lizard.py` are covered by the complete
  [Apache-2.0 text](Lizard-1.24.0-lizard.py-Apache-2.0.txt).

## Pygments 2.18.0 Erlang lexer

The Erlang reader also contains a modified TypeScript translation of the recorded
`pygments/lexers/erlang.py` range from Pygments 2.18.0. Its source header and the
provenance inventory identify that BSD-2-Clause material; the complete text is
[Pygments-2.18.0-BSD-2-Clause.txt](Pygments-2.18.0-BSD-2-Clause.txt).
